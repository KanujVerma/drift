# Roadmap

This is an outcome-oriented direction, not a schedule or a grant to run the
system. [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) records product decisions;
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) distinguishes existing components from the
intended system and its limits. GitHub issues and pull requests carry current
work and evidence rather than a duplicated status board.

## Priorities

1. **Make the product direction usable.** Complete the scoped #672 documentation
   and workflow realignment, reconcile conflicting active instructions and
   verify the integrated revision. [#668](https://github.com/KanujVerma/drift/issues/668)
   remains open and incomplete; do not repeat broad cleanup or represent its
   unresolved financial, source and safety defects as fixed.
2. **Make offline evidence and safety trustworthy.** Reuse existing evaluator,
   accounting, provenance and risk components; address relevant known defects
   before relying on them. Preserve point-in-time inputs, replay, retained source
   evidence, accurate money and cost accounting, and protections against
   untrusted content. Resolve universal risk enforcement, ambiguous execution
   outcomes and reconciliation before connected operation. Do not promote
   exploratory results by relabeling them.
3. **Build a useful portfolio-manager workflow.** Under separately scoped work,
   connect user suggestions and independent discovery to grounded research,
   thesis and portfolio proposals, daily research, lightweight continuous
   monitoring and event-driven reviews. Learn from measured outcomes inside the
   approved mandate; escalate out-of-scope ideas. Start with conversation and
   add a dashboard only if evidence justifies it. Reuse existing paths rather
   than building a second evaluator or account ledger.
4. **Establish credible investment and economic evidence.** Compare
   risk-adjusted results with the S&P 500 and report investment returns separately
   from the costs of operating the system. Use appropriate out-of-sample and
   prospective evidence, disclose data and cost gaps, and do not claim a proven
   edge from simulation alone. Source rights and any promotion-grade evidence
   remain subject to their applicable qualification requirements.
5. **Evaluate models only when separately authorized.** The future candidate
   set is GPT-6.1 Tera Extra High and GPT-6.2 Sol Extra High, followed by Hermes
   only if justified; Jev and other models are deferred. Compare usefulness and
   cost without assuming any result. This roadmap does not authorize model
   evaluations, provider calls or spending.

## Operational phases are separate decisions

- **Offline development and simulation:** Use authorized local and synthetic
  evidence with its limitations. Offline results are not connected paper
  evidence, live readiness or permission to use a provider.
- **Paper validation:** A future goal, subject to explicit authorization for
  any broker connection or external provider access, an approved mandate,
  appropriate controls and prospective evidence. Simulation alone does not
  complete it.
- **$100 pilot:** Future intent only, after paper validation and a separate
  explicit real-money authorization. The figure is not permission to fund an
  account, connect, place orders or spend money.
- **Further autonomy:** Consider only with validated evidence, an authorized
  mandate and separate permission for the proposed operational scope. No phase
  advances automatically because code exists or a previous check passed.

The exact risk thresholds, provider rights and budgets remain unset where not
approved. The old [D04 and D10](architecture/adaptive-experiments/decisions.md)
weekly-review cadence and ETF-first experimental framing are historical, not a
binding product roadmap. Preserve applicable technical safeguards and the
history of prior work rather than treating it as invalid.
