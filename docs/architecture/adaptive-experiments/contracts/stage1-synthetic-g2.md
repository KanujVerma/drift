# Controlled local-synthetic G2 composition

## Scope and authority

Issue #570 composes the installed K1 producers, K4 learning, original prepared
account lifecycle, M0 runner and existing M2 accounting engine. It supplies
executable engineering contracts for repeated decisions on the same account.
It is not an accepted provider interface, a numerical production mandate,
historical scientific qualification, an external origin credential, a broker
adapter, or permission to trade. Existing authority flags remain false.

The threat boundary is a configured trusted local host and installed Python.
Only the installed B3/B4/B5 strategy families execute. Model/source output is
validated data, never arbitrary Python. The host supplies the SQLite root,
issuer instance and clock independently of submitted model content. A privileged
host capable of replacing its own root, monkey-patching installed Python or
rewriting all original evidence is outside this proof. Hash consistency alone
never authenticates an external issuer. Producer/consumer release acceptance
is recorded separately on the actual reviewed revision, not manufactured by an
API Boolean or by a successful test.

## Public entry points and retained evidence

`execute_controlled_campaign` in `drift.loop.stage1_controlled_campaign` consumes
one original prepared account through one original M0 invocation. Its inputs
include the complete immutable E1 campaign, arm and account manifest, original
trial provenance, prepared engine/account, original synthetic research context,
explicit mandate, checkpoint schedule and configured clock policy. It never
allocates fresh cash at a checkpoint or on restart.

The versioned `drift-local-synthetic-g2/1` profile joins, without modifying their
original identities:

| Record | Original evidence and ordering |
| --- | --- |
| Manifest | Full E1 account and campaign fingerprints, original allocation and witness, scheduler observation, exact data/cost/protocol/admission/evaluator/source hashes, complete schedule and policy before the account invocation |
| Observation | Detached actual marked book and session trace, committed by the original evaluator before a decision |
| Input witness | Original decision context, same-arm matured learning snapshot and actual observed availability before grant issuance |
| Grant | Exact checkpoint, logical request, attempt, input witness, validity window and budget reservation |
| Revocation | Original grant reference and explicit effective time, retained without rewriting the grant |
| Research | Actual producer-emitted start, cost, raw validation bytes and result, terminal attempt and complete output; no later fabricated validation result |
| Decision | Actual installed-family intent, original account/context, prior/proposed/staged family and reasoned selection, retention, refusal or unavailability |
| Execution | Detached existing atomic accounting preview at the actual next open; mandatory exact-policy approval/refusal/containment before committing any fill |
| Terminal | Original M0 identity, result association and classification, never a successful label for a failed or uncertain invocation |

The full Stage 1 profile checks that its first and last synthetic session dates,
253/126 preparation windows, scoring-session labels, monthly decision rule,
next-open execution and explicit mandate match the original E1 declaration.
The selected arm must use its declared scripted or conventional selector mode.
Development-only profiles remain distinctly labelled and cannot stand in for
that full-window proof. This verifies declared synthetic clocks, not exchange
holidays or historical source qualification.

The original account is single-use at the invocation level. Within that one
invocation, cash, holdings, claims, fills and fees are carried across checkpoints.
Original account/M0 records, operational IDs, financial run identity and derived
scientific projections remain different objects with different responsibilities.

## Explicit clocks and original validation

Economic decision cutoffs and operational observation instants are separate.
The source bundle defines synthetic economic sessions. The configured host
records when it actually observes the original allocation witness, context,
validation and outputs. The profile does not relabel a fixture clock as an
appointed operational wall clock or as historical first availability.

A grant is single-use for its declared logical attempt. Not-before, exclusive
expiry and effective revocation are checked at original attempt start. A late
completed proposal cannot authorize a switch, and the next-open authorization
checks its validity again. Equal operational timestamps retain original append
order; reversed dependency order is invalid. Failed or committed-but-unacknowledged
writes do not authorize retries, refunds, reopening a spent capability or account
reallocation.

Both `produce_proposal` and `select_conventional_candidate` have optional
validation-observation callbacks. They deliver detached original raw output,
invocation binding and the actual `ValidationResult` immediately after the real
validator call, before terminal completion. The default preserves existing
producer contracts. A failed callback propagates without inventing completed
retention. The durable campaign recorder retains attempts and costs as emitted,
including failures and no-candidate outputs.

## Mandate and execution semantics

`SyntheticMandateV1` requires explicit currency, per-security and class caps,
gross and turnover limits, current-price freshness, pause and execution-knowledge
state. There are no guessed Balanced defaults. Decimal inputs are finite;
comparisons use exact rational arithmetic. Shorts, leverage, foreign securities,
missing prices, unresolved execution and unsafe existing holdings refuse or
contain independently of model confidence and approval mode.

A rejected incoming proposal preserves actual incumbent quantities. It cannot
suppress an independent current-book breach or turn unknown state into a safe
hold. This slice does not invent automatic liquidation or a new remediation
policy. The original evaluator computes the atomic accounting outcome exactly
once. The observer approves that exact detached preview before it is committed;
a close-time mandate verdict is insufficient if next-open prices change.

The existing M2 rebalance commits all planned fills or none. The `PARTIAL` and
`UNKNOWN` mandate fixtures verify containment of declared unresolved execution;
they do not demonstrate staged orders with genuine partial fills and an
unresolved remainder. The separate P05 producer acceptance vector remains
unproved by this atomic simulator. Supporting it requires an explicit execution
contract and original fill/reconciliation evidence, not fabricated fill rows.

The active strategy changes only after actual approved execution. Refused or
unexecuted candidates never inherit incumbent returns as their own feedback.
K4 consumes only completed earlier same-arm observed intervals; future, foreign,
sealed and substituted feedback is rejected by replay.

## Replay, journal completeness and costs

`read_controlled_campaign` in `drift.loop.stage1_controlled_readback` uses one
verified immutable original ledger prefix. Every join uses that captured prefix,
including original account and result helpers. Caller-made writer objects and
account capabilities are neither required nor recreated. The returned report
pins the original prefix tip and every accounted controlled-event hash. It also
requires the original installed Python source inventory: a different installed
revision cannot certify old decisions merely because its outputs happen to
match. The original pinned environment and source must be restored for replay.

The declared manifest, not successful result rows, defines the opportunity set.
Every relevant event is assigned exactly once to an allowed phase or an explicit
campaign-level record. This includes entries after a terminal result, orphan
references, contradictory envelope/body identities, and unknown versions in the
target namespace. Legitimate independent campaigns in a shared ledger are not
mistaken for corruption. Missing due opportunities remain unresolved even when
there are no retained rows for them. Future opportunities remain not yet due.
A legitimate no-research-attempt record differs from research followed by no
execution. Neither a matching hash nor a successful neighboring opportunity
can conceal a missing or contradictory original lifecycle. Every original
execution-authorization trace event requires exactly one retained execution
record; missing records keep the affected trial and whole campaign unresolved.
Original research or no-attempt completion must precede its consuming decision
in append order even when all operational timestamps are equal. Genuine late
cost supersession remains permitted and cannot retroactively authorize a decision.

Late accepted operating-cost corrections remain immutable new originals. For
an estimate 8 superseded by actual 9, the later projection charges 9, not 17;
the earlier as-of projection remains 8. Unknown costs remain unavailable, not
zero. Verified zero requires an original receipt. Trading costs are already in
net account values and are never subtracted again as operating costs.

`stage1_trial_projection` returns exact interval economics, using reduced
rational returns when a decimal would require rounding. Self-rehashed incorrect
arithmetic, contradictory cost states and unsupported completeness claims refuse.
A complete account interval does not make an unknown operating cost known.

The derived trial outcome `RETAINED` distinguishes an original retained-incumbent
decision with approved next-open execution from a mandate `REFUSED` outcome.
Research `hold`, `abstain` and `unavailable` retain their distinct original
`RESEARCH_*` reasons, costs and nullable selected family. This does not invent a
new target, suppress the incumbent strategy's ordinary internal decisions, or
make missing execution evidence complete. Actual fills remain those in the
original trace; a no-order hold is not a refused mandate. This additional value
belongs to the unmerged derived projection contract, not the frozen evaluator
schemas or retained original journal records.
A prefix ending at a retained or selected decision before its next-open
execution remains `UNRESOLVED` with `ORIGINAL_EXECUTION_UNAVAILABLE`. Absence of
that later original is not evidence of a refusal. An original close-time
mandate refusal remains separately identifiable even before the next open.
When a selected proposal is rejected at the next open, the derived refusal
keeps the original execution authorization's reason, such as expiry before
execution, instead of the earlier successful research-selection reason.

The campaign report separately includes the whole scoring interval, beginning
before the initial B3 execution, so the initial period is not omitted from the
comparison. It reuses existing metric math for economic gain and maximum
drawdown. Filled notional and original trading costs are reported separately.
`compare_controlled_campaigns` cold-verifies both roots and original allocations,
requires the declared complementary B/R arms and comparable data/cost/mandate/
execution/schedule, including each original grant's not-before, expiry and
revocation policy, and records both immutable prefix tips. Two accounts in one
paired run are not multiple independent samples merely because they have many
monthly checkpoints. No Sharpe, significance, profitability or superiority
claim is inferred from an insufficient synthetic sample.

## Compatibility and versioning

No-observer runs retain their original trace schema and bytes. The original
`EvaluatorTraceEventV1`, `EvaluationTraceLogV1` and `EvaluationRunArtifactsV1`
schemas remain frozen. Opt-in observation/authorization events use a distinct
`EvaluationTraceLogV2` admitted only by the existing V2 artifact container.
Ordinary V1 artifacts cannot silently accept the new event kinds. No golden
fingerprint is updated to accommodate this feature.

Original M0 RESULT associations preserve the existing schema-2 single-event
retention for canonical artifacts of at most 4 MiB. Larger canonical original
artifacts are retained using an additive schema-3 association in the same
configured local append-only ledger. Each original artifact is zlib-compressed,
then divided into at most 256 original chunk events of at most 256 KiB binary
payload each. The decoded complete canonical original is bounded to 128 MiB;
total compressed evidence is bounded to 64 MiB. The terminal association binds
the exact compressed and original lengths, SHA-256 hashes, count, original chunk
event identifiers and every ledger-event hash. Every chunk also binds its own
index, count, content digest, actual allocation and original M0 event. The
independent cold reader must account for every chunk in the one captured ledger
snapshot and enforce the original M0 -> chunk -> unique M0-use reservation ->
RESULT chronology before bounded decompression and full canonical validation.
Unaccounted/orphan chunks, truncated or malformed streams, missing artifacts,
foreign origin or inconsistent original result/trace/book hashes fail closed.
If any append is interrupted, the already consumed account cannot be retried,
refunded or reallocated. A schema-3 record cannot claim the schema-2 inline
bytes, and no schema-2 retention limit is silently raised.

The engine, portfolio accounting and baseline algorithms remain authoritative.
There is no second backtester, financial ledger, source qualification system,
provider credential or live connector. Original environment pins, locked
packages, schema fingerprints and historical fixture hashes remain unchanged.

## Verification matrix and declared limitations

The executable tests cover original producer callback chronology; pre-commit
approval/refusal/containment and detached custody; correct frozen V1 schemas;
complete journal membership including trailing grant/input/no-attempt records;
wrong scope; interrupted append before commit and lost acknowledgement; actual
cold readback in a fresh Python process; original cost correction/as-of history;
expiry, revocation, budget and history refusals; timeout and malformed output;
hold/abstain; pause, position/class/gross/turnover limits; UNKNOWN/PARTIAL
containment; and distinct-account comparison including the initial B3 period.

The full-size fixture uses twelve fictional assets and the first 456 genuine
XNYS scheduled dates from a pinned, offline-normalized 461-session calendar
read covering 2024-01-02 through 2025-10-31; the 456th date is 2025-10-24.
The date/open/close tuples were independently matched to `exchange_calendars`
4.13.1 for all 461 sessions (the captured 64-bit comparison fingerprint is
`aac013f21d5a422f`), including early closes, holidays and the January 9, 2025
special closure. The retained SHA-256 fixture is read from
`tests/fixtures/stage1/xnys_461_normalized_calendar_v1.json`. One original M1d
replay context holds its complete `ClosedWorldSessionCoverageV1`, the generated
open/explicit closed session rows, exact declared source and the selected
source-policy binding; the original `require_evidenced_clock_density` guard is
unchanged. Missing/contradictory coverage remains INDETERMINATE or refused.
The fixture retains the 253-session warmup, 126-session observation, B3 initial
execution and August/September actual month-end B4/B5 decisions on carried books.
The initial decision is July 8 at index378 and scoring begins July 9 at379.
The 21-session boundary is August 6 at399, so July 31 at395 is ineligible.
The complete eligible monthly sequence is August 29 at416 and September 30
at437, with actual next-open executions on September 2 and October 1.

This is a **normalized structured Alpaca calendar read**, not retained raw
provider HTTP response bytes, official historical exchange attestation or a
point-in-time 2024/2025 source vintage. The schedule data is captured after the
simulated dates, while asset prices, the open-price boundary calculations and
operational clocks remain fictional. All findings are retrospective,
exploratory engineering evidence and cannot be promoted to historical study
quality, original producer issuer acceptance, statistical scoring or trading
permission. The scripted AI and fixed conventional scores are deterministic
engineering fixtures, not an accepted real AI or frozen scientific R formula.

Live test results, exact source hashes, current CI and acceptance belong in the
issue/PR checkpoints. A test defined here is not a claim it has passed. Full G2
release requires completed exact-source verification and applicable independent
producer/consumer contract acceptance. G1/G3 historical qualification and G6
operational permission remain separate and are not granted by this document.
